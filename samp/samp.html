What Makes Indian Cuisine Unique

Indian cuisine is defined by layered spicing, regional diversity, and long culinary history.

Spices such as turmeric, cumin, coriander, ginger, and cardamom form the backbone of most dishes.


Indian food

Indian food is a diverse collection of regional cuisines—from creamy Punjabi curries and tandoori dishes to coconut‑rich Kerala seafood and rice‑lentil staples of Tamil Nadu—shaped by geography, history, and centuries of spice trade.



1.Kerala

Kerala food is a coconut-rich, spice-forward cuisine known for dishes like Sadya, Karimeen Pollichathu, Appam with Stew, Puttu–Kadala Curry, and Malabar Biryani, reflecting the state’s coastal geography and multicultural history.


Must try kerala dishes

  • Sadya:A vegetarian feast of 20–28 dishes served on a banana leaf, central to festivals like Onam.
  • Beef Fry with Parotta: A Kerala classic, especially in Malabar
  • Malabar biriyani:Fiery red curry with fish tamarind (kudampuli)
